**simplestcode** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy applies to all mobile applications published by simplestcode on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

## Information We Collect

Our apps **do not collect, store, or transmit** any personal information. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any form of identification. Our apps have no analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or third-party tracking of any kind.

## Advertising

Our apps contain **no advertisements** of any kind.

## Payments & Purchases

Some apps may offer optional features through a one-time in-app purchase. Any paid features are clearly and visibly indicated within the app before purchase. There are **no subscriptions, no recurring fees, no trial periods that convert to paid plans, and no hidden charges**. All payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) directly; we never have access to your payment information.

## Data Sharing

Since we collect no data, we do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

## Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. However, because we collect no data at all, children can use our apps with the same privacy protections as any other user.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review it periodically.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at **privacy@simplestcode.io**.
